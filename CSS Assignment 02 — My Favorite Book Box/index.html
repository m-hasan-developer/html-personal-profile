The $100 Startup

Book Name: The $100 Startup

Author: Chris Guillebeau

Published Year: 2012

Genre: Business & Entrepreneurship

About the Book

The $100 Startup is a book about starting a small business with very little money. Chris Guillebeau shares real examples of people who turned their skills and ideas into successful businesses. The book shows that you do not always need a lot of money to start something of your own.

Why I Like This Book

I like this book because it gives simple and practical ideas about starting a small business. It motivates me to use my skills to create opportunities and become independent. The book also shows that a small idea can become a successful business with proper effort and planning.

“You don't need a lot of money to start a business.”
